5. Rights and their implementation under the CCPA

As a California resident, you have the following rights under the CCPA:
5.1. Right to information
The User has the right to request information about the personal data collected by the Administrator, including:

  • Categories of Personal Data collected.
  • Categories of sources from which Personal Data is collected.
  • The business or commercial purposes for collecting, selling, or sharing Personal Data.
  • The categories of third parties with whom Personal Data is disclosed.
  • The specific pieces of Personal Data collected.

The User may exercise this right no more than twice within a 12-month period.
To exercise this right: The User may send a letter to the Administrator's email address with a request regarding the data collected by the Administrator.
With regard to data collected by Google LLC by the AdMob service, the User may use the following link, which will allow them to export their data.
5.2. Right to erasure
The User has the right to request the erasure of their Personal Data.
To exercise this right: The User may use the following link and follow the steps to delete the data collected by Google LLC. If the User wishes to delete data sent to the Administrator by email, they may send a request to the Administrator's email address.
5.3. Right to rectification
The User has the right to request the rectification of inaccurate Personal Information.
To exercise this right: The User may use the following link and follow the necessary steps to change the data collected by Google LLC. If the User wishes to correct data sent to the Administrator by email, they may send a request to the Administrator's email address.
5.4. Right to opt out of “sale” or “transfer”
If the User wishes to opt out of the ‘sale’ or “transfer” of data, they must delete the Mobile Application from their device.
5.5. Right to restrict the use of Personal Information
The Administrator does not collect Personal Information within the meaning of the CCPA. Personal information may be collected by the Administrator only if the User provides it on their own initiative in the process of sending an email to the Administrator. However, if the User wishes to restrict the use of Personal Information by AdMob service, they must delete the Mobile Application from their device.
5.6. Right to non-discrimination
The User has the right to non-discrimination by the Administrator for exercising their privacy rights under the CCPA. This includes the right not to be subject to retaliation for exercising their rights under the CCPA.
